    How to read SMD codes

    3-Digit Code

    First two digits are the significant value, third digit is the multiplier (power of 10). May represent a resistor or a ceramic capacitor.

    103 → 10 × 10³ → 10kΩ / 10nF

    4-Digit Code

    First three digits are the significant value, fourth digit is the multiplier. Used for precision SMD resistors only.

    1002 → 100 × 10² → 10kΩ

    EIA-96 Code

    Two digits map to a value from the EIA-96 table, followed by a letter multiplier. Used for 1% precision resistors.

    01C → 100 × 100 → 10kΩ
